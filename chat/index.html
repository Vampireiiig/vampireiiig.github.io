    Royal's War Tutorial

    Chat: use Global Chat to talk with online players. You can delete your own messages.

    Map: drag the map to move around and use the zoom buttons or mouse wheel to zoom. Click a state to inspect it.

    Land: attack enemy states with soldiers. Winning captures the state and its resources.

    Days: one day lasts 12 hours. You can manually advance after 6 hours. Population may grow, food/water are consumed, and there is a 1% chance one person joins your soldiers.

    Oil rigs: build for $1000, upgrade for $500 up to level 5. Each level pays $10 every random 35-70 minutes, with offline income capped at 50 hours.

    Maintenance: oil rigs need $100 maintenance every 7 days. If overdue, every harvest can fail. Failure starts at 10% and rises by 2% each overdue harvest attempt until you maintain the rigs.

    Trading: trade money, food, water, soldiers, population, and oil rigs with other players.
